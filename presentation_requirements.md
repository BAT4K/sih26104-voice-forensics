# SIH 2026 Idea Presentation Format Requirements

Based on the provided `SIH2026-IDEA-Presentation-Format.pptx` file, your team must adhere to the following slide structure and strict submission guidelines for the hackathon presentation.

## Required Slide Structure (Maximum 6 Slides)

### Slide 1: Title Page
Must include all of the following details:
- **Smart India Hackathon 2026**
- Problem Statement ID
- Problem Statement Title
- Theme
- PS Category (Software/Hardware)
- Team ID
- Team Name (as registered on the portal)

### Slide 2: Idea Title & Proposed Solution
- Describe your Idea/Solution/Prototype.
- Detailed explanation of the proposed solution.
- Explain exactly how it addresses the problem.
- Highlight the innovation and uniqueness of the solution.

### Slide 3: Technical Approach
- **Technologies to be used:** Programming languages, frameworks, hardware (e.g., PyTorch, Next.js, FastAPI, gRPC).
- **Methodology and process:** Provide flow charts, architecture images, or details of your working prototype (e.g., the 4-tier forensic pipeline).

### Slide 4: Feasibility and Viability
- Analysis of the feasibility of the idea (is it practical to build and scale?).
- Potential challenges and risks (e.g., 8kHz telephony degradation, latency).
- Strategies for overcoming these challenges (e.g., training with AMR-NB degrader, ECAPA speaker verification).

### Slide 5: Impact and Benefits
- Potential impact on the target audience (e.g., preventing telecom fraud for banking customers).
- Benefits of the solution across social, economic, or environmental factors (e.g., restoring trust in digital communications).

### Slide 6: Research and References
- Details and links to any reference material or research work you relied upon (e.g., ASVspoof datasets, Wav2Vec2 papers, Mahalanobis distance research).

## Important Submission Instructions

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Formatting & Limits**
> - **Maximum length:** Exactly 6 slides (including the title slide).
> - **Format:** You MUST save and upload the file as a **PDF**. PPT, Word Doc, or any other formats will NOT be supported on the portal.

> [!WARNING]
> **Design Guidelines**
> - Avoid long paragraphs. Present your idea using bullet points, diagrams, infographics, and pictures.
> - Keep explanations precise, easy to understand, and emphasize why the idea is unique and novel.
> - You **must use the provided template format**—do not change the section headers or pointer requirements across the 6 slides.
> - Slide 7 in the provided PPTX is just an instruction slide and should be deleted before you export the final PDF.
